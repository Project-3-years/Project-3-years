# Project 3 Years: Pre-Dev

## What Is Pre-Dev?

Pre-dev is the stage before development begins.

The purpose of pre-dev is to prepare for development by figuring out what needs to be made, learning the skills required to make it, planning the project, testing ideas, and creating documentation.

**Pre-dev is not development.**

It is the preparation that happens before development starts.

---

## Main Goal

The main goal of pre-dev is to prepare **Project 3 Years** for development.

By the end of pre-dev, I want to have a better understanding of:

- **What the game is**
- **What the player should experience**
- **How the story should work**
- **What needs to be programmed**
- **What technology may be needed**
- **What I need to learn**
- **How development should begin**

---

## Current Goals

### Story

- Develop the basic story structure.
- Define the purpose of each chapter.
- Plan Chapter 0.
- Determine what the player should experience and understand.
- Develop the philosophy behind the game.

### Programming

- Continue learning C#.
- Understand the code I write.
- Learn the programming concepts needed for the project.
- Experiment with basic systems.
- Avoid relying on copy-and-paste solutions that I do not understand.

### Preparation

- Set up the project structure.
- Decide what tools and technologies may be needed.
- Identify the systems that will need to be developed.
- Plan the first stage of development.
- Create documentation for important ideas and decisions.

---

## Pre-Dev Prototype

A small terminal-based prototype may be created during pre-dev to test ideas and prepare for development.

**This prototype is not the first development version of the game.**

It is an experiment used to understand how certain systems and story ideas could work before development begins.

The prototype may include:

- Player input
- Variables
- Basic dialogue
- Choices
- Basic branching
- Saving information
- The beginning of Chapter 0

**The prototype does not need to represent the final quality of the game.**

Its purpose is to help prepare for development.

---

## Documentation

During pre-dev, important ideas and decisions will be documented.

Documentation may include:

- Story ideas
- Character information
- Chapter plans
- Programming notes
- Technical experiments
- Development decisions
- Problems and solutions
- Things that still need to be figured out

The documentation is meant to help me understand the project, prepare for development, and keep track of its progress over time.

## Moving Into Development

Pre-dev will end when the project is prepared enough for development to begin.

This means having a clearer understanding of:

- **The project’s direction**
- **The first chapter**
- **The required tools and technology**
- **The systems that need to be developed**
- **The first development goals**

The exact point where pre-dev ends may change as the project develops.

**The goal is not to finish the game.**

**The goal is to prepare for development.**

---

## Philosophy of Pre-Dev

Project 3 Years is a long-term project.

I do not need to know everything before development begins.

I need to learn, experiment, make mistakes, understand what went wrong, and prepare as much as possible.

Pre-dev exists to turn an idea into a foundation for development.

> **The goal of pre-dev is not to make the game.**
>
> **The goal is to prepare to make the game.**
